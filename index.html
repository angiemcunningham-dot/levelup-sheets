The three conditions & the Golden RuleRead them

1. One-Hour Candle Confirmation

  • Long: a 1H candle must open and hold above both the Simple Moving Average (SMA) and Smooth Moving Average (SMMA).
  • Short: a 1H candle must open and hold below both moving averages.

2. Momentum Alignment

  • Momentum indicators must point in the direction of the trade on the majority of timeframes.
  • 1H timeframe is absolute — non-negotiable.
  • The two timeframes below the 1H must align with the trade direction.
  • Higher timeframes must be curving, or beginning to curve, in the direction of the trade.

3. Clear Path to Target

  • Long: no major higher-timeframe resistance between entry and target.
  • Short: no major higher-timeframe support between entry and target.
  • Check the 4H, 8H, 12H, and Daily timeframes.
  • No major higher-timeframe level should block the path to your target.

🚨 Golden Rule

  • All three conditions must be met before entering a trade.
  • Triggered does not mean confirmed.
  • Confirmation comes before execution.
  • If even one rule fails — no entry.

  • Both sides of a setup now show. When Chelsea publishes a long and a short on the same asset, the board used to show only one of them. Now you get one card for the asset, and the other side opens in a dropdown on its detail page. Nothing she published is hidden any more.

  • A banner if automatic tracking falls behind. The TRIGGERED / TP ticks update every 30 minutes. If that stops, a red banner now says so. On 20 October it stopped for almost 13 hours and the board said almost nothing — that is what this is for.
  • Old watches move aside. A setup still “watching” after two weeks without triggering now sits in a collapsed section instead of the live list. Nothing is deleted — it is still there, with its age stated.
